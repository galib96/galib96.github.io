## University of Tennessee, Knoxville

### Graduate Teaching Assistant

---

#### CBE 350: Chemical and Bioengineering Reactor Fundamentals
**Fall 2023, Fall 2024, Fall 2025**

An undergraduate course covering the fundamental principles of chemical and biological reactor design and analysis, including:
- Reactor Design Principles (Batch, CSTR, PFR)
- Reaction Kinetics and Rate Laws
- Mass and Energy Balances in Reactors
- Catalysis and Enzymatic Reactions
- Bioreactor Design and Operation
- Scale-up and Optimization of Reactor Systems

**Responsibilities:**
- Conducted weekly recitation sessions for 30+ students
- Held office hours to provide individual support and clarify complex concepts
- Graded homework assignments, quizzes, and exams
- Assisted in developing problem sets and exam questions
- Provided detailed feedback to help students improve their understanding

---

#### CBE 555: Elements of Synthetic Biology and Metabolic Engineering
**Spring 2025**

A graduate-level course focusing on the principles and applications of synthetic biology and metabolic engineering, including:
- Synthetic Biology Design Principles
- Metabolic Pathway Analysis and Engineering
- Flux Balance Analysis and Metabolic Modeling
- Strain Design and Optimization Strategies
- CRISPR and Genome Editing Techniques
- Modular Cell Design and Genetic Circuits
- Applications in Biomanufacturing and Biotechnology

**Responsibilities:**
- Facilitated discussion sessions on current research topics
- Mentored graduate students on computational projects
- Assisted in grading research paper reviews and project presentations
- Provided guidance on metabolic modeling software and tools
- Helped students develop research proposals for course projects
